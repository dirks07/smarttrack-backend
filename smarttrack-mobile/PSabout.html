SmartTrack Equipment Monitoring System

An automated, website-based monitoring and requisition platform designed for Colegio de Montalban students, instructors, and property custodians to borrow, dispatch, and track school assets with barcode accuracy.

Read Guidelines First Get Application File ↓

How the System Works

Follow these 4 simple steps to request, receive, and return equipment safely.

1

Submit Online Ticket

Students and faculty log into the portal, select the item (projector, HDMI, presentation clicker), quantity, and purpose, then submit a request.

2

Custodian Review

The Property & Custodian office reviews availability. Once marked Approved, the ticket transfers to the physical dispatch desk.

3

Counter Pickup

Show your student or employee ID at the Custodian Terminal. The officer scans the physical barcode/asset tag and releases the item.

4

Inspection & Return

Return the asset immediately after your class or presentation. The custodian checks the item condition before closing the ticket.

Rules & Guidelines for Borrowers

Ensure smooth transactions by reviewing the custodian policies below.

✓

Official School Identification Required

Always present a valid CDM Student Identification Card or Faculty ID when claiming reserved items at the Property & Supply counter.

✓

Care and Proper Handling

Inspect equipment upon pickup. Ensure cables, lenses, and remote accessories remain intact and undamaged throughout your usage.

✓

Prompt Return & Condition Audit

Return borrowed assets promptly within the designated schedule so the next class or faculty member can use them without delay.
